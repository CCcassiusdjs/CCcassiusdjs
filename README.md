## Research

**Jundiá** (CNPq/MCTI/FNDCT ITI-A No. 25/2025, Laboratório de Sistemas Autônomos, PUCRS, Apr 2026 to Mar 2027).
Simulation platform for offshore autonomous vessels with digital-twin support.
I build fault-tolerance benchmarks and ROS 2 integration on Team B (Fault Tolerance and Integration).
Principal investigator: Prof. Anderson Domingues. Partners: Loughborough University, INSTOR.

**SleepSensing** (FINEP and FAPERGS, Laboratório de Sistemas Embarcados, PUCRS, 2025 to present).
Sleep-apnea prediction from polysomnography, SpO₂, ECG and thoracic-effort signals.
I write the Python ETL pipelines, extract spectral features (FFT, wavelets), and train four
classifier families (random forest, gradient boosting, CNN, LSTM).

**AIM-FTA** (CNPq 407477/2022-5, Laboratório de Sistemas Autônomos, PUCRS, May to Nov 2025, closed).
Fault injection in ArduPilot SITL to measure silent data corruption in the EKF3 estimator
(24 states, multi-core). 3,794 runs produced 59 critical silent data corruptions, and pitch
carried corruption in all 59. Estimated failure rate: 31.17 FIT (failures per 10⁹ hours).
Principal investigator: Prof. Ney Laert Vilar Calazans.
