## Research

M.Sc. student in Computer Science (PPGCC) at **PUCRS**, embedded systems research line,
advised by Prof. Dr. César A. M. Marcon and co-advised by Prof. Dr. Anderson R. P. Domingues.
Affiliated with the **Laboratory of Autonomous Systems (LSA)** and the
**Laboratory of Embedded Systems (LSE)**.

The work centres on **validating cyber-physical systems** — building simulation and digital-twin
infrastructure faithful enough that a fault found in silico is a fault that matters at sea.

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Jundiá** | Advanced simulation platform for autonomous vessels in offshore environments |
| **Digital Twins for AUSVs** | Modelling and validation of autonomous unmanned surface vehicles |
| **SleepSensing** | Machine-learning prediction of obstructive sleep apnea from biomedical signals |

Fault injection and reliability assessment in avionics software, and mixed-reality
applications for inclusive education, round out the research lines.
